Engr. Patricia Asemota

Director

As a director at Honatrix Nigeria Limited, Engr. Patricia Asemota plays a pivotal role in shaping sustainable practices and fostering resilience within our projects. With over a decade of experience in Civil and Environmental Engineering, Patricia embarked on her journey as a Project Engineer at The Architectural Balance Associates and Specks Project Nigeria Ltd. Over time, she ascended to the position of Senior Engineer in the Physical Planning Division at the University of Benin.

Patricia’s academic background, which includes a degree in Civil Engineering and a Master’s in Water Resources and Environmental Health Engineering, equips her with a comprehensive understanding of sustainable practices crucial for mitigating the impacts of climate change. Her expertise lies at the intersection of construction and environmental sustainability, where she excels in optimizing energy efficiency and promoting occupant health through innovative building envelope advancements.

In recent years, Patricia has shifted her focus toward team development, ensuring the company’s sustained operations and providing solutions for sustainable real estate development. She seamlessly integrates climate change resilience strategies into construction management, ensuring that buildings not only meet sustainability standards but also remain robust in the face of environmental challenges.

Beyond her professional endeavors, Patricia enjoys traveling, biking, watching movies, networking, and mentoring students and graduates. Her commitment to fostering a community of informed and skilled sustainable engineering professionals is truly commendable.
