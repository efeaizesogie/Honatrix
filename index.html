    About Honatrix Nig. Ltd.

    Real Estate and Civil Engineering Since 2016

    Honatrix Nig. Ltd. commenced operation in June 2016, registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission with certificate number RC 1242021. We are a Real Estate Development Company established to carry on general contracts, civil engineering, building construction and investment in real estate. We are specialized in luxury homes and commercial properties, serving both local and foreign investors with a reputation built on trust, transparency and an exceptional level of customer service.
