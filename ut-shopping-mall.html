    Frequently Asked Questions

    Who are the developers?

    Honatrix Nigeria Limited is the developer while West Marc Nigeria Limited is the promoter of the plaza.

    When can I schedule a site visit?

    Site visits can be scheduled for every day of the week. Contact us to fix a perfect timing.

    What documents do I get after payment?

    • Receipt of purchase (if payment complete)
    • Invoice on instalment payment (till balance is completed)
    • Letter of offer, stating agreed terms
    • Contract of sales

    What facilities are provided in the Plaza?

    All en-suite shops, serviced with central security, 24/7 electricity, ample parking lot, easy accessibility, standard international electrical fittings, water treatment, and many more.

    Other fees to be paid?

    There are no other charges as the price is all inclusive. However, there will be service charge upon physical allocation.

    When will shop allocation be done?

    After final payment, date of completion is 12 months.
