Ajumuka Henry

Managing Partner & Civil Engineer

Henry is the managing partner and also a versatile Civil engineer in the outfit. He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering (B.Eng), from the University of Benin, Benin City and has various certifications in project management and occupational health and safety.

He has been very active in the civil engineering construction industry and building technology, with specialty in real estate development. He started his carrier in 2010 with Extreme Engineering Ltd (NDDC Contractors) as an IT staff.

He has worked in the property department of Jubilee Mortgage Bank, Port harcourt branch where he participated in the development of Whispering Palms Estate and Glory Land Estate. He has participated in the development of residential properties in Lagos while working as resident engineer with Tripple A Consults. He later joined CFL Group also in Lagos as a project engineer, where he managed the construction of shopping plaza/malls on Lagos mainland and Lekki area of lagos state.

Real Estate development, civil engineering construction, facility management
